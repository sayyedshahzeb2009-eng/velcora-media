06 — FAQ

Questions,
answered.

What kind of websites can you build?

We can create premium business websites, landing pages, e-commerce stores, SaaS websites, hospitality sites, portfolios, real-estate sites and custom interactive experiences.

Can you make the site responsive?

Yes. Every project is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile, with touch-friendly interactions and performance in mind.

Can you add 3D and animation?

Yes. We use motion and 3D selectively to create a memorable experience without turning the website into a slow gimmick.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. Velcora is positioned for clients in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and Europe, with remote collaboration built into the process.
